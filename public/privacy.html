Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Dot Dial for Android handles information when you use the app, including its optional Google sign-in and Google Drive backup features.

Information handled on your device

Depending on the features you use and the Android permissions you grant, Dot Dial may access contacts, call history, phone-call functions, microphone input for applicable features, and app preferences. The app uses this information to provide dialling, contact, call-history and personalisation features. Contacts and call-history information used by the app is stored in the app's local data unless you choose an export or backup feature.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Dot Dial uses Google authentication through Firebase Authentication to establish your signed-in account. Google provides account authentication. Dot Dial does not ask you to give it your Google password.

Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional. If you choose to authorize it, Dot Dial requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This scope allows the app to create and access files that the app creates or that you explicitly open with it; it is not a request for unrestricted access to every file in your Drive.

When you start a backup, the app can upload a JSON backup to a folder named Dot Dial Backups in your Google Drive. Depending on the backup option you select, the backup may contain contact names and phone numbers, contact details and notes, call-history entries (including numbers, direction, timestamps and duration), and app settings. The app can list and download its backup files when you use restore. The backup is stored in your Google Drive account, under Google's terms and privacy practices. The app developer does not operate a separate server to store these Drive backup files.

Do not back up information unless you have the right to store it. Protect your Google account and review the contents of any backup you create.

Sharing and service providers

Dot Dial does not sell your personal information. Information is shared with Google when you use Google sign-in or authorize Google Drive backup, as needed to provide those features. Google processes that information under its own privacy policy and service terms. Android and device manufacturers may separately process information under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app, subject to Android and device backup behavior. Drive backup files remain in your Google Drive until you delete them from Drive. You can revoke Dot Dial's Google access from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Revoking access prevents future Drive operations until you authorize again; it does not automatically delete files already stored in Drive.

Permissions and your choices

You can manage Android permissions in your device settings. Some app features may not work if their required permissions are denied. Google Drive access is optional and requires your consent through Google's authorization screen.

Children's privacy

Dot Dial is a general-purpose phone utility and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service connected to the app, contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

Security

Dot Dial uses Android permission controls and Google's authorization mechanisms for the features described here. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device and Google account protected, and avoid sharing backup files or account credentials.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Please review this page periodically.
