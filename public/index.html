Android dialer

A dialer with its own character.

Dot Dial brings calling, contacts, call history and a distinctive dot-inspired interface together in one Android app.

Made for everyday calling

  • Dialling, contacts and call-history tools.
  • Personalisation options, including themes and caller backgrounds.
  • Optional backup and restore features, including Google Drive when you choose to connect it.
  • Android permissions are requested for the features that need them.

Your data and backups

Dot Dial's optional Google Drive backup lets you choose to upload supported app data to a folder in your own Google Drive. Read the Privacy Policy for details about the data included and how the integration works.

Google account access is requested only when you choose to connect the relevant Google feature. You can review or revoke third-party access in your Google Account settings.
